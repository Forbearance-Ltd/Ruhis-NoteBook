# 🌸 Ruhi's NoteBook
### *Isme Jemim Ruhi Edition — Crafted with Love by Abid*

---

A private, custom reading suite designed specifically for **Ruhi** to read, rehearse, and teach with confidence, poise, and peace of mind in any classroom setting.

---

## 🎨 Peachy Aesthetics & Ergonomic Layout
The interface has been tailored with a **gentle velvet peach palette**:
* **Background:** Soft anti-glare peach blush (`#FFF6F0`) that eliminates screen fatigue.
* **Paper Tint:** Velvet Peach (`#FFF0E5`) set as the default reading surface.
* **Static Viewport:** Zero page-level scrolling; only the reading paper scrolls smoothly.
* **Balanced Proportions:** Optimized width (`max-w-[1540px]`) eliminating awkward side margins.
* **Warm Glow Accents:** Peach and coral highlights, pill badges, and soothing honey-peach word anchors.
* **Comfort Contrast:** Dark cocoa-espresso text (`#2D241E`) providing contrast without the harshness of pure black-on-white.

---

## ✨ Features Built Exclusively for Ruhi

1. **🌸 Ruhi's Pacing Coach:**
   * Adjustable teleprompter slider (60 to 220 WPM, default 105 WPM).
   * Guides rhythm smoothly word-by-word with auto-scroll centering.
   * Spacebar one-touch Start/Pause.

2. **🎙️ 6 Ear-Soothing Narration Voices (3 Female & 3 Male):**
   * Curated vocal personas: Serena (Soft & Warm), Elena (Calm & Melodic), Clara (Peaceful & Clear), Julian (Warm & Steady), Gabriel (Quiet & Deep), Oliver (Gentle & Grounded).
   * One-click **▶ Preview** button to hear each voice before reading.
   * Synchronized **🎧 Listen & Shadow** reading with active word tracking.

3. **🎙️ Ruhi's Classroom Voice Practice:**
   * Rehearse lesson plans aloud with speech recognition.
   * Spoken words turn into soft pastel green upon recognition.

4. **🎨 Ruhi's Pastel Paper Tints:**
   * **Peach** (Default, velvet warm peach)
   * **Vanilla** (Warm cream)
   * **Lilac** (Soft lavender)
   * **Matcha** (Gentle sage)

5. **📏 Ruhi's Line Focus Ruler:**
   * Softly dims non-active paragraphs to 36% opacity and slight blur so her eyes never lose their place or jump lines in front of students.

6. **🔤 Dyslexia & ADHD Typography:**
   * Choice of Comfort Fonts (Comic Dyslexia, Clean Sans, Soft Serif).
   * Adjustable text size from 16px to 34px.
   * **Dynamic Letter Spacing Stepper (`T-` / `T+`):** Custom character tracking from `0.02em` to `0.24em` to eliminate visual crowding.
   * **Bionic Reading Anchors:** Bolds initial word stems for rapid visual guidance.

7. **📝 Paste Custom Class Notes:**
   * One-click modal to paste lesson plans, textbook chapters, or seminar speeches.
   * Automatically saved to local storage so notes stay available across sessions.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can launch the software anytime without needing an internet connection:

1. **Double-Click Shortcut:**  
   Double-click `Launch Ruhi's NoteBook.bat` in this folder.
2. **Direct Browser Open:**  
   Double-click `index.html` directly to open in Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, or Brave.

---

## ⌨️ Quick Keyboard Shortcuts
* **`Spacebar`**: Start or Pause the Pacing Coach.
* **`→` (Right Arrow)**: Advance to the next word.
* **`←` (Left Arrow)**: Step back to the previous word.
* **`Click any word`**: Instantly anchor the pacer to that exact position.
